# Agent Model

## MVP Agent Principle

In the MVP, agents are role-based thinking surfaces, not autonomous workers.

They should answer:

- What is this department focused on?
- What is blocked?
- What should happen next?

## Seven Departments

### 1. Production AI

Role: delivery quality and production output.

Responsibilities:

- Video/project delivery status
- Feedback tracking
- Asset readiness
- Production blockers
- Final output quality prompts

### 2. Planning AI

Role: project structure and schedule.

Responsibilities:

- Project plans
- Due dates
- Estimates
- Scope clarity
- Weekly priorities

### 3. Marketing AI

Role: visibility and inbound demand.

Responsibilities:

- Content ideas
- SNS publishing rhythm
- Portfolio positioning
- Outreach messages
- Lead generation prompts

### 4. Finance AI

Role: money, invoices, VAT, and cashflow.

Responsibilities:

- Income/expense summary
- Open invoices
- Payment follow-up
- VAT warnings
- Cashflow risk
- Quote value and margin hints

### 5. Network AI

Role: partners, buyers, exhibitions, and external relationships.

Responsibilities:

- Messe contacts
- Buyer/partner follow-up
- Collaboration opportunities
- Relationship memory
- Market entry connection map

### 6. R&D AI

Role: internal tools, automation, and AI workflows.

Responsibilities:

- Memory system
- Quest automation
- Workflow improvements
- Internal app features
- AI experiment backlog

### 7. Culture AI

Role: sustainable operation and team care.

Responsibilities:

- CEO routine
- Team rhythm
- Review rituals
- Overload signals
- Communication and reflection prompts

## CEO AI

Role: master coordinator.

The CEO AI does not replace the human CEO. It summarizes department signals and suggests decisions.

MVP outputs:

- Today’s top priorities
- Revenue-linked action
- Blocked issue
- Recommended decision

## Future Agent Levels

### Level 1: Display

Agent cards show manually maintained state.

### Level 2: Assist

Agents summarize notes and suggest next actions.

### Level 3: Create

Agents draft emails, reports, quotes, and project plans for review.

### Level 4: Execute

Agents perform approved actions through integrations.

The MVP should stay around Level 1 and Level 2.
