# BridgeMakers AI OS Development Instructions

Before planning or changing this repository, read `BRIDGE_CONSTITUTION.md` and treat it as the highest-level product and safety contract.

- Do not weaken approval boundaries, audit history, evidence traceability, or human authority.
- AI may draft constitutional or policy changes but must not activate them without explicit human approval.
- New automation must declare its trigger, inputs, outputs, permissions, approval gates, failure behavior, and idempotency strategy.
- Unknown or ambiguous external actions default to human approval.
- Preserve existing accounting behavior and user changes unless the requested work explicitly changes them.
- Keep BridgeMakers Services and Fair Tale as separate business workspaces over shared skills and policies.
